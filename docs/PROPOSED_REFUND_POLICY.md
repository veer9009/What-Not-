# DRAFT — PENDING BUSINESS APPROVAL

Proposed What Not? cancellation and refund policy, 25 November 2026.

**This document has not been approved by the business owner. It is not an active customer policy, seller agreement or authorization to enable prepaid UPI.** Prepaid UPI remains disabled. Any operational targets below are proposals requiring approval and implementation before publication.

## Proposed responsibilities

The seller fulfils the order and receives any payment directly. What Not? records orders and seller confirmations; it does not hold funds, operate a payment gateway or automatically issue refunds. The business owner must approve the seller's refund obligations and the platform's complaint and escalation responsibilities. Statutory consumer rights must not be reduced by a seller's individual terms.

## Cancellation and payment

- Buyers may request cancellation through the existing order controls where the order state allows it. Preparation, delivery and payment are separate records. Cancellation does not itself prove that money was returned.
- An unpaid COD cancellation ordinarily has no prepaid amount to return. If a seller has actually collected money, that collection remains recorded and must be addressed separately.
- For a cancelled, rejected, unfulfilled, incorrect, defective or otherwise disputed paid order, the seller must assess the buyer's request and explain the proposed remedy, including the refundable items, delivery fee and amount. Business approval is required for category-specific eligibility, evidence, cancellation charges and exceptions. Do not publish a blanket exclusion for food, services or delivery fees without review.
- Each seller in a multi-seller checkout remains responsible for that seller's order and funds. A dispute with one seller does not silently cancel or refund another seller's order.

## Proposed handling targets — not active commitments

The owner should approve a complaint acknowledgement target of 48 hours, a reasoned decision target of seven calendar days, and a seller refund initiation target of seven calendar days after approval. Actual credit timing depends on the external payment provider. These proposed targets must be checked against applicable legal duties and bank/provider rules before adoption; they do not replace any shorter mandatory deadline.

The seller should document the order ID, original amount and method, agreed refund amount and reason, initiation date and external reference. A reference alone does not prove receipt. The buyer should confirm actual credit, and unresolved or failed transfers should be escalated. The current application does not provide a refund ledger or automated reconciliation; this procedure requires an approved operational record and support process before prepaid launch.

## Privacy, safety and complaints

Never request a UPI PIN, banking password, OTP or card credentials. Use only the minimum order and transaction evidence needed; restrict it to the buyer, assigned seller and authorized support staff. Do not publish private destination details or complaint attachments. Refunds must not require buyers to authorize a debit or scan a payment-request QR.

Before publication, the business owner must supply the responsible legal entity, grievance officer/contact, support channel, escalation steps, record-retention schedule and buyer-facing complaint acknowledgement process. A complaint must not automatically mark an order paid, delivered or refunded. Seller payment confirmation is a seller statement, not bank verification. An order receipt is not a tax invoice or proof of payment.

## Mandatory approval and activation gates

1. Business owner and appropriate legal reviewer approve the final policy, eligibility, amounts, deadlines, exceptions, grievance responsibilities and seller obligations.
2. Publish the approved version, record its version/date/location and ensure buyers can review it before confirming a prepaid order.
3. Approve and operate seller payment-destination review and change control. Administrative review is not bank account verification; changed details revoke approval.
4. Complete isolated simulated-payment tests and separately approve production activation. The disabled-by-default UPI flag and approved-policy reference must both be explicitly configured, and each destination must have current approval.

**A draft file, a synthetic test-policy reference, an admin review or passing tests does not satisfy business approval or authorize a real transfer. Do not enable prepaid UPI from this document.**
